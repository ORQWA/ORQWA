Map tiles for finding the countries a route passes (#230), as cached by `orqwa-osm`
(`<zoom>/<x>/<y>.pbf`): OpenFreeMap vector tiles in the OpenMapTiles schema, cut down to the
borders between countries (`boundary`, `admin_level` 2) and the countries' labels (`place`,
`class` country) to keep them small. 6/33/22 covers Switzerland and its neighbours, 10/533/357
and 10/533/358 Basel, 4/8/5 labels the countries of Europe. © OpenFreeMap, © OpenMapTiles,
data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL 1.0).
