# Credits

Third-party assets in ORQWA, with their sources and licences (R47, ADR 0009). Only own work,
CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA are allowed.

The app's settings show these credits and every licence notice of the software built into it
on their About tab (R83, ADR 0018): ORQWA's GPL, the Godot engine and its components, FFmpeg's
libraries (LGPL 2.1 or later) and every Rust crate, from `core/orqwa-app/notices/`.

## Models

Urban concrete and paving textures are own work, baked from the procedural materials in
`art/textures/build.py` (GPL-3.0); sidewalks, curbs and building surroundings are generated
by `core/orqwa-world/src/urban.rs`.

City benches and bins reuse the own-work models generated by `art/props/build.py` (GPL-3.0).

Building and vegetation models and the textures (`app/assets/models/buildings/`,
`app/assets/models/vegetation/`, `app/assets/textures/`) are own work, generated by the
Blender scripts in `art/` (GPL-3.0 like the rest of ORQWA); see
[art/README.md](../art/README.md).

The spreading and upright fern models are also own work, generated by
`art/vegetation/build.py`; their leaflets are geometry using the shared frond material.

The riders' bodies (`app/assets/models/riders/`) are derived from the realistic bodies and
skeleton of [Human Base Meshes](https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/) v1.4.1 by
**Blender Studio** and contributors, **CC0 1.0**; the extract is kept in
`art/sources/human-base-meshes/` (see its `SOURCE.md`). Their kit, helmets and bikes are own
work, generated by `art/riders/build.py`.

## Data

Map and terrain data credits are shown in the app and stored in every course file; see
[Courses](https://github.com/ORQWA/ORQWA/wiki/Courses).

The sample course ORQWA ships (`core/orqwa-gd/courses/twann-chasseral.tqc`, R95) holds such
data: map tiles © OpenFreeMap © OpenMapTiles, data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL 1.0), and
terrain by Mapterhorn (CC BY 4.0) and AWS Terrain Tiles.

The list of ISO 3166-1 country codes that matches the map's three-letter codes to two-letter
ones (`core/orqwa-osm/src/iso.rs`) is taken from Debian's
[iso-codes](https://salsa.debian.org/iso-codes-team/iso-codes) (`data/iso_3166-1.json`,
LGPL 2.1 or later). The test tiles in `core/fixtures/countries/` are cut from OpenFreeMap
tiles: © OpenFreeMap, © OpenMapTiles, data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL 1.0).
