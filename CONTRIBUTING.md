## Contributor License Agreement

Before your first pull request can be merged, please accept the
[Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). A bot asks you on the pull request; you sign with your
GitHub account and your full name. You only do it once, and it covers your earlier
contributions too.

You keep the copyright in your work. The CLA lets the maintainer add permissions to the GPL
without asking every contributor again: for example an exception so ORQWA can ship through app
stores, or one for combining it with a company's separately licensed integration (CLA
section 2). Your work stays under the GPL, version 3 or later.

If you submit something that isn't your own work, such as vendored code, models or map data,
name its source and license in the pull request. It stays under that license and the CLA
doesn't cover it. Work made with coding agents or other AI tools counts as yours if you
directed and reviewed it and the tool's terms don't restrict its output. So do commits a bot
makes on your behalf. [ADR 0045](docs/adr/0045-contributor-license-agreement.md) explains why.
